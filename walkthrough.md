# MediKiosk Admin Portal Streamlining Walkthrough

## Summary of Changes

In response to user requirements, the **Admin Portal** was completely refactored to retain **ONLY** the 4 requested core capabilities and eliminate all extraneous features (such as patient lists, doctor registries, server node monitors, profile tabs, and mock controls):

1. **Number of Patients Used**:
   - Computes total unique patients who have interacted with MediKiosk (registered accounts, patient profiles, and checkup summaries).
   - Displayed as a large KPI card with live auto-detection indicators.

2. **Number of Doctors**:
   - Computes total verified medical practitioners on duty in the hospital registry.
   - Displayed as a dedicated clinical registry KPI card.

3. **Number of Health Summaries**:
   - Computes total clinical health summaries and OP tickets generated by MediKiosk.
   - Displayed as a dedicated AI triage KPI card.

4. **AI Dynamic Graph (Auto-Detecting & Self-Updating)**:
   - **Auto-Detection Engine**: Continuously aggregates real-time patient visits, OP tokens, and AI triage summaries by date.
   - **Interactive Timeframes**: Toggle between **7 Days**, **14 Days**, and **30 Days**.
   - **Dynamic Views**: Toggle metric views between **Checkups & Summaries**, **Total Patients**, and **AI Triage Runs**.
   - **Dynamic SVG Vector Chart**: Renders smooth glowing curves, filled area gradients, interactive bar columns, peak traffic badge, and detailed hover tooltips.
   - **Auto-Detected AI Narrative Insights**:
     - Auto-calculated Growth Rate (`+100%` or current velocity).
     - Auto-detected Peak Load Window (e.g. `14:00 - 16:00`).
     - Auto-classified Top Clinical Specialties (e.g. `General Medicine (42%)`, `AYUSH (35%)`, `Pediatrics (13%)`).
   - **Live Background Auto-Sync**: Automatically polls every 10 seconds with a visible countdown badge (`10s`) and a manual **AI Sync Now** button.

5. **Extraneous Features Removed**:
   - Removed tab navigation (`patients`, `doctors`, `profile`, `system`).
   - Removed patient data tables and search filters.
   - Removed doctor directory tables.
   - Removed server nodes, CPU/memory telemetry, and mock accreditation cards.
